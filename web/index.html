How it works

From forecast to crew, before the rain.

Four steps run ahead of the storm, so the fix lands while it is still cheap.

01

Watch the sky

Pull the Sofia rain forecast. A heavy-rain window arms the system and starts the clock.

Open-Meteo
02

Scan every river

At each point along the rivers, an AI compares the live satellite view against the map and flags channels choked by debris, dumping, or overgrowth.

Esri imagery · OpenStreetMap · vision model
03

Model the flood

Pull the real terrain and spread the backed-up water over the low ground — to see which blocks and roads actually go under, and how deep.

Copernicus GLO-90 · bathtub inundation
04

Send the crew

Rank every site by the euros it prevents, then write the crew a plan: what to clear, what gear to bring, and by when.

Data & method

Public data, stated plainly.

Every layer is open and checkable. No black boxes.

OpenStreetMapriver network, buildings, roads, hospitals & stations
Esri World Imagerysatellite view for each inspection point
Open-Meteorain forecast and terrain elevation
Copernicus GLO-90digital terrain for the flood model
EU JRC · Huizinga 2017depth-damage curves, Bulgaria-adjusted

Flood depth is a first-order estimate from rainfall and the measured blockage; the spread is a bathtub model over real terrain, not a full hydrodynamic solve. Terrain, imagery, assets and damage curves are real — the water physics is a transparent MVP, and we say so.
